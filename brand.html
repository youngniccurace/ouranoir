Noir, Gold & Azure.

Luxury that speaks the language of creators. Gold says value. Azure says connection. Noir keeps it discreet. Every colour has one job — and they never compete for it.

The Idea

Warm Value, Cool Connection

Our audience lives on creator platforms and social media, where blue means trust, technology and community. They come to us for growth and earnings, which gold has signalled for centuries.

Gold (hue 36°) and azure (hue 199°) sit almost opposite each other on the colour wheel, so each makes the other look richer. And azure isn't borrowed: our Noir is already a blue-black (hue 210°). Azure is that same blue, brought into the light.

We deliberately soften the platform blue (less neon, a little deeper) so it matches gold's calm, champagne tone instead of shouting over it.

Gold36° Azure199° Noir210°

Near-complementary pair: maximum contrast, natural harmony. Noir shares azure's hue.

The Logo

The Aura Ring

“Oura” sounds like aura — the glow around someone. The gold ring is that aura and the O of Oura; with the N it reads ON. The azure glint is where the aura catches the light: the spark we add to every creator we work with.

Using the logo

  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the N free on every side.
  • Minimum size: lockup 120 px wide on screen (30 mm in print); symbol alone from 16 px.
  • Light or dark only: Deep Gold & Noir on light; Champagne Gold & Ivory on dark.
  • Social profiles: use the Midnight profile picture.

Never

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to the logo.
  • Change its colours, or place it on gold, azure or busy photos.
  • Retype it in another font — always use the files.
  • Move the glint or close the ring.

Photo Direction

Warm Light, Cool Screens

Choose photos that carry the palette: golden-hour skin tones and warm light (gold) next to phones, laptops and data on screens (azure). Real people, confident and in control — never explicit.
