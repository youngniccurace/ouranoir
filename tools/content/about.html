Our story

Founded by a creator who lived it.

Our founder is a creator herself. Before OuraNoir, she was managed by several agencies — and saw the same problems again and again: missed timing, inefficient operations and too little care for the creator behind the page.

With her partner, who didn't want to see creators treated that way, she started OuraNoir to build the agency she wished she'd had — improving everything she found missing.

Today a 70+ person in-house team across many nationalities and time zones runs everything: account management, chatting, content, analytics, marketing and traffic. Creators' accounts and data stay with our in-house team only. The system works so well that other agencies now white-label our chatting, marketing and traffic services.
