FAQ

Questions Creators Ask Us

Who can apply?
Anyone aged 18 or over with a valid government-issued ID. Whether you're just starting out or already have an established page, we'd like to hear from you.
Do I need a large following to join?
No. We work with new creators as well as experienced ones. Building an audience is part of what we do.
Do I keep ownership of my account and content?
Yes. Your account and your content always belong to you. You decide what you create and what your boundaries are.
How does OuraNoir get paid?
We work on a commission basis that is agreed with you up front and set out in your contract, so we only do well when you do. We'll go through the details on your intro call.
Is my application kept private?
Yes. Your details, photos and ID are only used to review your application and verify your age, and are never shared publicly.
How soon will I hear back?
We review every application and aim to reply by email within 2–3 business days.
